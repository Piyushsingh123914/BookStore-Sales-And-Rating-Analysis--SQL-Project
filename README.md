## Basic

* Retrieve the top 10 best-selling books by gross sales.
* Retrieve the books published in 2012 that have an average rating above 4.0.
* Identify the hidden gems: books with a rating above 4.0 but fewer than 1,000 units sold.
* Calculate the profit margin for each book (gross sales minus publisher revenue), ranked from high to low.
* List the top 5 most rated English books (language code `en`).
* Retrieve the books where the author rating is higher than the book's average rating.

## Advanced

* Calculate the average book rating for each genre, ranked from high to low.
* Calculate the total revenue for each publisher and rank the publishers using a window function (`RANK`).
* Find the authors who wrote more than one book, along with their number of books and average rating, using a CTE (`WITH` clause).
* Calculate the total units sold and total gross sales for each publishing year.
